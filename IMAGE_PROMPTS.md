# SEIRIN — prompts for image generation

Промпты ниже написаны на английском: так генераторы изображений обычно точнее соблюдают материал, камеру и архитектуру. Каждый блок предназначен для копирования целиком.

---

## Общие технические правила для фонов

```text
Wide 16:9 visual novel background. No people unless explicitly requested, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no subtitles. Leave a clean unobstructed lower-central area for dialogue box and character sprites. Use coherent physical perspective, straight architectural geometry, believable materials, and consistent light direction. Avoid AI artifacts: warped frames, melted objects, duplicate furniture, impossible cables, asymmetrical windows, distorted hands, unreadable pseudo-text.
```

```text

Her whole body is made only of pale-cyan flowing transparent water: the checkerboard background is clearly visible through her head, face, torso, arms, hair and lower streams. No opaque skin, no white mask, no pink, no milky liquid, no plastic, no jelly, no clothes. Her form is created by elegant cascading water layers, smooth contours and refracted highlights. Her lower body dissolves below the knees into separate flowing water streams; no lower legs and no feet. Long hair consists of smooth liquid tendrils without suction cups. Add small transparent heart-shaped water bubbles and a tiny clear drinking glass balanced upright on her head. Huge gentle kind eyes are transparent glass-like water lenses with very faint cyan refracted iris rings; the checkerboard remains visible through them. Add a tiny transparent-water nose and subtle nostrils. The mood is cute, naive and affectionate, with physically plausible high-transmission water, refraction and caustics.
