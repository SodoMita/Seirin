Outputs: `kitsune_v2_festival_style.png`, `stella_v2_no_quadcopters.png`

## Kitsune — ukiyo-e / flat-graphic poster style
**KEEP.** Deliberately different rendering from the v1 flat-VN look: bold ink
outlines, flat colour blocking, gold-leaf accents, poster composition. Proves
the roster does not have to share one style.
- The nine booms now spring from the HIP yoke and fan wide past her outline
  with open gaps between them — the v1 halo-around-the-head problem is fixed.
- Reads unmistakably as MOUNTED EQUIPMENT: telescoping tubes, buckles, harness
  at the waist, cyan ring lights. The 2.1 reconciliation is now visual, not
  just written.
- Remaining: paws read as bare animal feet under the hem; the ring lights
  should burn cyan ONLY while live-streaming, so a non-streaming variant needs
  them dark.

## Stella — owner-flagged quadcopter fix
**KEEP.** The literal drone bodies are gone; the swarm is now pure fine points
of light, which is both prettier and more legible.
- Wings now read as FLAT overlapping translucent planes, like panes of coloured
  glass, not feathered anatomical wings. This also fixes the black-fill defect
  logged in SILHOUETTE_TEST.md, where the wings filled as solid anatomy in
  violation of her banned array.
- Two ground projectors with cables taped across wet stone are visible at the
  bottom edge — the visible-hardware rule satisfied without any drone bodies.
- Remaining: the single unlit point is not findable at this scale; it needs to
  be larger or closer to a bright cluster to register.

## Technique promoted
"Tiny points like distant stars or fine glowing dust" + an explicit ban on
drone/rotor/propeller BODIES gives the swarm read without the ugly hardware.
Show the mechanism with the PROJECTORS on the ground instead — the hardware
rule is satisfied by one visible source, not by making every emitter literal.
