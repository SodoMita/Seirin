# 02_lineup_groupB — Kitsune / Ryuki / Reika / Saya / Lumina

Model: Arena image tool (Nano-Banana class). Output: `lineup_groupB_v1.png`

**Verdict: KEEP WITH EDIT** — the strongest of the three sheets. Four of five
memory points landed on the first attempt.

## Kept
- **Kitsune**: the nine booms read as MOUNTED EQUIPMENT with visible ring lights
  at the tips, fanned with daylight between them, and hardware visible at the
  hip. This is the whole Kitsune reconciliation working in one image — the
  costume reads as a rig, not as anatomy, with no pixel of the v4 look lost.
- **Saya**: the eleven-vial cyan bandolier is excellent and instantly readable.
  Pen through the bun, glasses, tired-but-competent face, unretouched under-eyes.
  Best single figure across all three sheets.
- **Reika**: harness sits OUTERMOST over the jacket with real buckles, and the
  raw cut-out insignia hole is present in the chest webbing. Orange strap at hip.
- **Lumina**: ring light held in both hands at chest height with a real handle,
  warm gold lighting her face, secular tailored suit — no robe, no vestment. The
  priest-robe default was successfully refused.
- **Ryuki**: dragon head under the arm with an open jaw. Skin rendered with
  dignity — no wounds, scabs or scales. Safety pass clean.

## Broke
1. **Reika reads male.** Ash-grey undercut plus a heavy flight jacket pushed her
   past androgynous. She is a woman; the design wants lean strength and
   authority, not a masculine read. Highest-priority fix in this sheet.
2. **Kitsune's tails are too short and too high**, clustering around her head
   like a halo rather than fanning from the hip yoke. The yoke itself is not
   clearly visible; the booms appear to sprout from her shoulders.
3. **Ryuki's jacket lost its quilting grid** and reads as a plain green coat;
   her jade is muddied toward sage. The dragon head's three visible repairs are
   absent — it looks factory-new, which loses the object's whole history.
4. **Lumina's ring light is too small** — it reads as a hand mirror or lollipop
   rather than a lighting instrument, and the central hole is too small to work
   as the silhouette hole it is supposed to be at 64px.
5. Minor: Saya's lab coat is closed rather than open, so the broken-hem
   silhouette and its triangle of light are missing.

## Change for next attempt
One change per figure, per `iteration.md`. Priority order: (1) state Reika's sex
explicitly and soften the jaw while keeping the undercut and the muscle;
(2) move Kitsune's booms to spring from the hip yoke and extend them past her
outline; (3) add the three mismatched repairs to the dragon head; (4) make
Lumina's ring light large enough to frame her face.
