Key art of STELLA, a non-corporeal drone-swarm-and-projection art installation,
full figure, tall and elongated at about 8 heads.

BACKGROUND: a deep night-violet to indigo gradient sky over a rain-wet harbour,
with soft airborne haze and mist catching light, and blurred warm city lights
far below in bokeh. Rich, saturated, luminous. She is a light source, so the
ground must stay DARK for her to read — bright figure against deep violet.

SHE IS NOT SOLID. She is assembled ENTIRELY from THOUSANDS OF INDIVIDUAL POINTS
OF LIGHT hanging in the air — each point is one tiny hovering drone seen as a
brilliant glowing lens, like a swarm of luminous fireflies holding the shape of
a standing woman. Brilliant aurora violet (#A65FFF), luminous cyan (#4FE3FF)
and bright mint (#5FFFC0) where the light overlaps. Her surface is made of the
GAPS between these points — she is mostly empty space, and the violet sky is
clearly visible THROUGH her body everywhere.

HOW TO DRAW THE DRONES — most important: the drones ARE the points of light.
Draw them overwhelmingly as small brilliant glowing dots with a faint circular
rotor-blur halo around each, so the swarm reads as shimmering light. Only about
five or six of the NEAREST drones resolve enough to show a small delicate dark
quadcopter body inside its own glow, and those are drawn small, elegant and
crisply in perspective. Do NOT scatter opaque black toy quadcopters across her
body. Do NOT paste stock drone shapes on top of the figure. The aircraft are
revealed BY the light, not stuck onto it.

WINGS: behind her shoulders, wide fanned shapes made of FLAT OVERLAPPING PLANES
of projected light, like separate sheets of stage lighting cutting through haze.
They must be clearly BROKEN INTO SEPARATE GAPPED PANELS with dark sky visible
between every panel — never a solid continuous wing, never feathered, never
anatomical. Above her head a floating crown of separate unconnected points.

Her lower body does not end in legs: below the hips the points thin out and
TRAIL AWAY DOWNWARD into a long falling stream of separate sparks that
scatters into nothing above the ground. No feet, no ground contact, no cast
shadow.

Her face is barely there — two slightly brighter clusters of points where eyes
would be, no iris, no mouth. Serene and impersonal.

ONE SINGLE DRONE IS COMPLETELY DARK AND UNLIT — a small matte charcoal
quadcopter with no glow at all, clearly visible in perfect formation among the
shining ones, near her chest. It must be findable.

EXCLUDE: no text, letters, numerals, watermarks, signatures, logos, UI, borders.
No skin, no fabric, no clothing, no continuous outline, no solid body. No
anatomical detail and no sexualised rendering — she is a volume of light. No
angel, goddess, fairy or religious framing, no halo, no feathers. No real magic
— every glow is a manufactured light source. No dull or desaturated colour.
