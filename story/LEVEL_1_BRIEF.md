# Level 1 Hierarchy: Executive Narrative Brief (Refined Canon)
## Project: Seirin: Night Shift — Resonance 2030

### Executive Overview & Vision
This story hierarchy expands the franchise to its full thematic potential. Set in the coastal port city of Seirin (2032), the narrative starts with an **Ordinary Daily Prologue (Chapter 0)** where the Protagonist (GG) wanders the streets before any central conflict is triggered. Depending on GG's choices—whether to inspect the docks, visit Miya's apartment courtyard, stop by the teahouse, or simply stay home—the story branches into vastly different routes.

There is no forced heroism: if GG chooses to procrastinate or ignore the city's subtle signals, the world moves on without him, leading to distinct solo endings.

---

1. **Protagonist (GG)** — The player character; an observer in Seirin whose choices dictate whether events unfold or stall entirely.
2. **Miya Kagetsuki (Age 5)** — A curious 5-year-old child living in Tsukimachi near a gothic cathedral view window. She observes the street sweeper below and invites GG into "mystical occult rituals" and child-like magic games.

### Master Narrative Structure & The 5 Solo Routes

#### Chapter 0: The Ordinary Street & The Choice to Act
GG begins walking through Seirin's streets—past the port docks, the Tsukimachi alleyways, and the apartment building where 5-year-old Miya looks out her window at the eternal street sweeper. GG has full freedom of action. The story does NOT force an adventure.

#### The 5 Solo Routes (Одиночные руты ГГ):
1. **Solo Route 1 — Procrastination at Home (Прокрастинация дома):**
   - GG returns home, shuts the blinds, and ignores all incoming calls and news alerts. As days pass, Akatomi Dynamics and the Chorus of the Abyss activate the citywide Resonance Lattice unopposed. Seirin falls under corporate mind-shaping, resulting in a quiet defeat where GG remains unaware of what was lost.
2. **Solo Route 2 — Procrastination in Company & Self-Destruction (Прокрастинация в компании):**
   - GG hangs out with disillusioned local youth in Tetsuba's dark alleys, indulging in escapism, bad habits, and nihilistic apathy. The self-destructive lifestyle culminates in a tragic personal ending while the city collapses in the background.
3. **Solo Route 3 — Personal Success & Global Failure (Личный успех):**
   - GG focuses purely on personal career advancement—taking high-paying freelance coding jobs for Akatomi or Aquaforge. GG achieves wealth, a luxurious apartment, and personal success, but globally the city is transformed into a corporate dystopia.
4. **Solo Route 4 — Philosophical Reflection & 4th Wall Break (Философский слом 4-й стены):**
   - GG steps back from the conflict to engage in deep philosophical contemplation. He observes the characters as archetypes, breaks the 4th wall to address the player/reader, and attempts to influence the world directly. Realizing his attempts inside the script are in vain, he decides to break out of the narrative into a "more real reality," stepping into the unknown.
5. **Solo Route 5 — The Lone Fighter (Одиночная борьба):**
   - GG attempts to fight Akatomi, Chorus of the Abyss, and corporate enforcers completely alone without allying with any faction or character. This route is filled with dozens of rapid loss traps, bad endings, and overwhelming opposition.

### Character & Faction Special Routes

#### Miya's Route — The Mystical Rituals & The Cathedral Window
- **Concept:** 5-year-old Miya invites GG to participate in "magical spells," "ghost summonings," and secret rituals in her playroom and balcony overlooking the gothic cathedral. In a world without real magic, these rituals have no supernatural effect, but they create rich, imaginative adventures.
- **Outcomes:** GG can either support the corresponding faction or reject magic to fight against them. In both branches, the route ends with a **Happy Ending**, where GG and Miya accomplish something genuinely important for more than one faction.

#### AI Route — Stella & Splash (Стелла и Сплеш)
- **Concept:** Explores the boundary between artificial intelligence, physical soft-robotics, and human empathy. GG mediates between Saya's soft-robot Splash and Akatomi's networked art AI Stella, leading to digital transcendence and AI subjecthood recognition.

#### Faction & Romantic Arcs
- **Iron Requiem Arc (Reika & Ren)**: Heavy machinery salvage and industrial defense.
- **Aquaforge Arc (Saya & Splash)**: Environmental engineering and soft-robot ethics.
- **Chorus of the Abyss Arc (Lumina)**: Deciphering the acoustic projections.
- **Akatomi Corporate Arc (Kurogane & Momo)**: Corporate intrigue and frequency control.
