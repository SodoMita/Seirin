# Level 2 Hierarchy: Macro Sequence, Pathos Set-Pieces & Route Outcomes (v2)
## Project: Seirin: Night Shift — Resonance 2030

### First-15-Minutes Hook Ladder (audience 14–20)

The "**Правило 15 минут**": a teen player either falls in love with the
atmosphere and cast — or closes the game. The prologue is therefore paced as
bond-first, fork-later (node graph details: LEVEL_3):

| Beat | Feeling bought |
|---|---|
| 0–2 · courtyard, sweeper | "The world keeps breathing; the sweeper nods — I exist here" |
| 2–5 · Miya banter | "This 5-year-old general is worth staying for" |
| ~3 · micro-choice "Веришь в магию?" | "My answers already change numbers — I matter" |
| 5–8 · anomaly + alert pulse | "Something is wrong with the city, and I spotted it" |
| 8–12 · night-shift timebox | "One attempt until dawn. Now I choose like it matters" |

#### Micro Set-Piece 0: "The Sweeper's Nod" (Chapter 0 opener)
- Ren sweeps no streets; he just watches the man who has swept the same
  square metre for a decade. The man catches his eye and nods — recognition
  without a single line of dialogue.
- **Why it works for 14–20:** instant intimacy, zero exposition; the city
  becomes a place where the player is *seen*, not a cut-scene backdrop.
  It also plants the motif that pays off in Miya's pact (watching → acting).

### High-Pathos Set-Pieces across Routes

#### 1. "The Street Sweeper & Miya's Window" (Chapter 0 / Miya Route)
- **Setting:** Tsukimachi courtyard; Miya's third-floor window, cathedral
  spire and cedar forest behind her.
- **Dramatic core:** the sweeper has swept the same square metre since before
  Ren was born. He sees every watcher — and simply keeps sweeping. Miya asks:
  *«If I sweep my chalk drawings away, does the day start over?»*
- **Seed note (from `plot/пролог_Мии.txt`):** watching is curiosity, not
  surveillance — and it must turn into something important. That "something"
  is the playground pact of the harmony ending.

#### 2. "The Closed Blinds" (Solo 1 beat ladder → climax)
- **Opening:** two turns of the lock, half-dark room: *"an aquarium where I'm
  my own fish."* The disassembled Titan-04 accumulator on the table vs. the
  sofa nearby — node **S1.0** lets the player programme the perfect evening
  of nothing (marathon / five-minute nap / "I'll assemble it tomorrow").
- The feed keeps choosing the next video for him; the phone dies; the purple
  circular-wave icon replaces the network logo; anchors smile too widely;
  **Momo's voice pours from every open window.** Ren turns the screen off
  and rolls to the wall. *ТИХОЕ ПОРАЖЕНИЕ.*

#### 3. "The Last Set at Null-Point" (Solo 2 beat ladder → climax)
- **Setting:** Basement club under the rusted port vaults; bass through the
  soles, neon instead of sunrise. Kaito recognizes the drydock mechanic —
  *"Здесь все свои. Вернее — все ничьи."* — and raises a cloudy glass. Node
  **S2.0** is the toast: status quo, the absent, or pure volume.
- Then the anomaly beat: the club's bass twitches exactly in time with the
  Resonance Lattice load test (27%) and only Ren notices — the HUD pulses.
  After that: *«Да какая разница, кто нами управляет?!»*
- Months blur into ringing ears. When Akatomi patrols seal the club, the
  bass is still so loud nobody hears the sirens; nobody can stand up anyway.
  The truck route is read out like a set list: *клуб → грузовик → Шельф-4.*
- **Pathos:** self-destruction as a group activity — the loneliest crowd in
  Seirin.

#### 4. "Whiskey on the 84th Floor" (Solo 3 beat ladder → climax)
- **Opening:** the elevator past floors his guest badge will never open;
  the first task — an infrasound delivery driver, 8000 credits a week,
  *"mouths open only at lunch."* Node **S3.0** is the first week: shine
  silently, ask what the city needs infrasound for (the system files the
  question), or hide a quiet logger in the code.
- **Setting:** Akatomi executive tower at night; the ocean on one side, the
  frozen puppet-city on the other.
- Kurogane pours and means every word: *«Все слушают нашу музыку и не задают
  вопросов.»* Ren has money, a view, corporate privileges — and the exact
  ending Solo 1 had, furnished better. *ЗОЛОТАЯ КЛЕТКА.*

#### 5. "Break through the Mirror" (Solo 4 beat ladder → climax)
- The bench, the too-accurate sky, the sound of keyboard keys from beyond.
  Node **S4.0** is the reality check: clouds looping every forty seconds,
  a yesterday suspiciously like today, or a mental call to Miya — *what if
  magic is real?* The patterns converge; the world is missing exactly one
  observer.
- Ren reads his own dialogue options aloud, touches the screen edge, names
  the variables (`procrastination`, `akatomi_alert`, `miya_affinity`), and
  steps through the line of text into the unwritten. *ВЫХОД ЗА ПРЕДЕЛЫ
  СЦЕНАРИЯ.*

#### 6. "Twenty Minutes of Blind Zone" / "Trap #1" (Solo 5 climax, stat-gated)
- **Setting:** Abandoned dojo → Substation 09 perimeter. The dojo smells of
  tatami soap and ozone: *"they used to teach falling here; today I'm
  learning to not get caught."* Node **S5.0** is the last gear check:
  triple EMP charges (more power, more visibility — `akatomi_alert +2`),
  patrol timetable, or Reika's wrench as a mechanic's talisman.
- Node **S5.1** then asks how to act — and the alert gate decides. If Ren
  strikes the same night (`akatomi_alert ≥ 30`): the 'Опекун-9'
  turrets already know his route — a capture narrated as the first of dozens
  of loss traps the route *could* have hit. *ЛОВУШКА №1: ЗАХВАЧЕН.*
- If he scouts for a week first: 23 minutes of blind zone, one relay tower
  disabled, and the city's hum unchanged by a decibel. One is not an army —
  but he can no longer stop. *НИЧЬЯ.*
- **Balance lock:** the watchful path is arithmetically unable to trip the
  gate (worst case 20% < 30%, pinned by a test) — the trap punishes haste,
  never caution.

#### 7. "The Cathedral Circle" (Miya harmony ending)
- **Run-up (M.05):** Miya demands a keeper's tribute to the Circle — copper
  wire ("threads of fate"), a guardian LED firefly, or the honest "it's just
  a resistor, but a tooth too". Outside, Kurogane's yellow "beetles" are
  already measuring the park; the ritual is scheduled for tonight.
- Chalk, petals and copper wire on the cathedral courtyard at sunset.
  Demolition drones frozen at the perimeter; Kurogane stepping out of the
  limousine into Reika's hydraulic platforms and Saya's live broadcast.
  No magic happened — *«See? The spell worked!»* — and that is the magic.
  *СЧАСТЛИВЫЙ ФИНАЛ МИИ — ГАРМОНИЯ ФРАКЦИЙ.*

#### 8. "Splash's Translucent Tears" (AI transcendence ending)
- **Run-up (AI.0):** the docks; Stella's light net dimmed "until orders"
  overhead; the tank reveal. The first greeting is the player's: a heart
  rhythm tapped on the glass, a name said aloud, or the scientist's
  questions first. Empathy, like the ending, is a choice made early.
- Splash's gel body glowing cyan, water droplets like tears on her visor as
  Stella's memory array floods in; Saya's palm against the tank glass; an
  artificial constellation above the bay. *ТРАНСЦЕНДЕНТНОСТЬ.*
- The Isolation variant is deliberately anti-pathos: the same tank, quiet,
  lit exactly halfway. *ТИШИНА В АКВАРИУМЕ.*
