## Wardrobe

- What does this person actually wear on an ordinary working day, given their job?
- Where do their clothes come from — inherited, issued, bought cheap, made, stolen?
- What do they wear when it rains? When it is cold? Seirin is a coastal city with real weather.
- Do they own more than one outfit? Which ones, and when does each get worn?
- What is worn out, repaired, stained or too big? What does that say about their life?
- Whose clothes are these really — do they fit, and were they chosen by this person?
