## How to use this file

These are questions for the **project owner or a design agent** to
answer. The art agent does not answer them — it asks, waits, and then
executes. Record answers in `../assets/cast.json` under this
character's `answers`, and set `approved: true` once signed off.

The existing design document is an early AI-generated draft. Treat it as
a starting point to interrogate, not as canon. A better answer replaces
it — just record that it changed.

Wardrobe answers should draw on `../references/wardrobe-questions.md`,
which catalogues real garment traditions and names the three defaults to
avoid (priest-robe sci-fi, the featureless bodysuit, the one-outfit
character).

## Wardrobe

- What does this person actually wear on an ordinary working day, given their job?
- Where do their clothes come from — inherited, issued, bought cheap, made, stolen?
- What do they wear when it rains? When it is cold? Seirin is a coastal city with real weather.
- Do they own more than one outfit? Which ones, and when does each get worn?
- What is worn out, repaired, stained or too big? What does that say about their life?
- Whose clothes are these really — do they fit, and were they chosen by this person?
